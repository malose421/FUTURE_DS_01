# 📊 Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

## 📌 Overview
This project is part of my Data Science & Analytics Internship at Future Interns.

The objective of this task was to analyze customer data to understand churn behavior and provide actionable insights to improve customer retention.

---

## 🎯 Key Business Questions

- Why are customers leaving the platform?
- Which customer segments are most likely to churn?
- How long do customers stay?
- What strategies can reduce churn?

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Power BI (Dashboard & Visualization)
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Business Analytics

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- The overall churn rate is **27%**, indicating a significant portion of customers leave the service.
- Customers with **month-to-month contracts** show the highest churn.
- Higher **monthly charges** are associated with increased churn.
- Customers with longer tenure are more likely to stay.

---

## 🚀 Recommendations

- Encourage long-term contracts through discounts or incentives
- Offer pricing adjustments or loyalty rewards for high-risk customers
- Improve onboarding experience for new customers
- Monitor high-churn segments proactively
